# Metrics And Statistics

Primary metrics:

- Task success.
- Final output quality.
- Research-spine phase quality.
- Tool-call validity and unnecessary-call rate.
- Memory relevance and contamination rate.
- A2A success and interaction efficiency.
- Model-management score.
- Skills and system-prompt adherence.
- Token, cost, latency, and tool-call accounting.

Paired deltas should be estimated per scenario and aggregated with bootstrap confidence
intervals once sample size is sufficient. Current numerical results are `TBD-evidence`.
