# Research Questions

RQ1. Can Pi replace Istara's full agentic-management core while preserving all user-visible
Istara feature contracts through adapters?

RQ2. Under paired prompts, tools, memory inputs, model policy, and stopping rules, does Pi
produce equal or better research-spine quality than Istara's current engine?

RQ3. Does Pi improve tool-call validity, recovery, and efficiency per token and per tool call?

RQ4. Can Pi's provider/model layer cover Istara's routing, reasoning, usage, and secret-hygiene
requirements without bypassing Istara compute policy?

RQ5. Can Pi's harness/session/channel patterns cover Istara's A2A and messaging surfaces
without split-brain state or unsafe credential handling?

Every answer remains `TBD-evidence` until backed by run artifacts under
`comparison-Istara-pi/runs/`.
