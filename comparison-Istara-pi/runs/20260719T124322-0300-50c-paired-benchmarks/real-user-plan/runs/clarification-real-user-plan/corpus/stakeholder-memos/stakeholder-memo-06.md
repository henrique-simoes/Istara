# Stakeholder Memo 6

Owner: Patient Experience
Decision pressure: patients cannot tell which tasks are required before the appointment.

## Position
The stakeholder believes the redesign should prioritize let staff filter by next patient action instead of visit date only.

## Tension
This may conflict with give coordinators a one-click reason for overriding automation because teams have different views of readiness ownership.

## Evidence requested
- More separation between patient, caregiver, and staff workflows.
- Stronger proof that source trails reduce support calls.
- A clear privacy review before caregiver-facing launch.
