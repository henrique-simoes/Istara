# HB-JR-01 — Journey: chasing a late invoice

> SYNTHETIC FIXTURE. Built from interviews P01, P02, P05 + usability sessions + support tickets.

## Stage: Notice
- Doing: owner opens aging list Monday 7am
- Thinking: "who owes me, really?"
- Pain (with sources): deposits under different names (P01 Tue example)
- Opportunity: normalize payer display names
- Evidence: P01 interview; P02 interview; P05 interview.

## Stage: Nudge
- Doing: owner sends reminder from personal email
- Thinking: "will this sound desperate?"
- Pain (with sources): no in-app nudge; tone anxiety
- Opportunity: templated nudges with tone control
- Evidence: P01 interview; P02 interview; P05 interview.

## Stage: Follow-up
- Doing: second reminder + phone call
- Thinking: "am I harassing them?"
- Pain (with sources): no escalation ladder
- Opportunity: staged escalation with pauses
- Evidence: P01 interview; P02 interview; P05 interview.

## Stage: Resolution
- Doing: payment arrives Friday, mismatched reference
- Thinking: "which invoice was that?"
- Pain (with sources): manual matching
- Opportunity: smart match suggestions with undo
- Evidence: P01 interview; P02 interview; P05 interview.

## Stage: Learning
- Doing: owner updates spreadsheet ritual
- Thinking: "next month will differ"
- Pain (with sources): no closed loop
- Opportunity: receivables retrospectives
- Evidence: P01 interview; P02 interview; P05 interview.
