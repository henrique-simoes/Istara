# HB-COMP-02 — Competitor benchmark: Foundry & Co

> SYNTHETIC FIXTURE. Assembled from public marketing pages + scripted trial walkthroughs (no credentials, no scraping). Evaluated against the same 10-task Harbor protocol as our usability sessions.

## Positioning angle: branch-first onboarding with video verification.

### Task 1 vs theme: Auto-matched bank feeds: loved by owners, distrusted by accountants (PLANTED DIVERGENCE)
The override log is where trust lives. If I can see who confirmed what and undo it, I'll let it guess.

Rival behavior: requires three more steps than Harbor.

### Task 2 vs theme: Payroll timing vs cash reality
Holiday weeks compress everything and nobody warns you. Last Thanksgiving I learned by overdraft.

Rival behavior: matches Harbor exactly — parity, not advantage.

### Task 3 vs theme: Spanish statements translate words, not meaning
The Spanish version says 'cargo por sobregiro' where the English says 'overdraft protection transfer'. Those are not the same thing and my customers notice.

Rival behavior: matches Harbor exactly — parity, not advantage.

### Task 4 vs theme: Branch handoff drops context at the counter
Privacy theater: I can't see their online steps 'for security', but I can see their balance. Explain that logic.

Rival behavior: requires three more steps than Harbor.

### Task 5 vs theme: Fraud alerts: ops wants more, owners feel spammed (PLANTED DIVERGENCE)
Digest mode exists, they told me, buried in settings I need a map to find. Discovery is part of the product.

Rival behavior: matches Harbor exactly — parity, not advantage.

### Task 6 vs theme: Accountant collaboration runs on screenshots
Permissions are all-or-nothing, so I choose nothing and pay in screenshots. The pricing page should say that.

Rival behavior: completes cleanly in under two minutes.

### Task 7 vs theme: Mobile deposit limits surprise growing businesses
Bottom line: tell me the limit before I need it, raise it when my history earns it, and never surprise me.

Rival behavior: passes but hides the limit until submit.

### Task 8 vs theme: Invoice chasing eats the morning
Bottom line: if the app can't tell me who is late and nudge them for me, it's a viewer, not a tool.

Rival behavior: matches Harbor exactly — parity, not advantage.

### Task 9 vs theme: Receipt capture dies at the point of sale
Thermal paper fades. By March the January receipts are blank ghosts and I'm reconstructing from card statements.

Rival behavior: requires three more steps than Harbor.

### Task 10 vs theme: Approval chains stall on one absent person
Deputy coverage exists on paper. In practice the deputy can't see the attachments, so they approve blind or not at all.

Rival behavior: completes cleanly in under two minutes.

### Task 11 vs theme: Auto-matched bank feeds: loved by owners, distrusted by accountants (PLANTED DIVERGENCE)
I always worry that a wrong match sits there looking correct. Correct-looking wrongness is the most expensive kind.

Rival behavior: fails the Spanish variant.

### Task 12 vs theme: Payroll timing vs cash reality
Three times last quarter I moved money between accounts at midnight to make morning payroll. That's not banking, that's parkour.

Rival behavior: matches Harbor exactly — parity, not advantage.

## Verdict: best handoff continuity; slowest approval chains; alert granularity praised by ops personas.
