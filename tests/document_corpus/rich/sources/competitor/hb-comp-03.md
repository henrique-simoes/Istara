# HB-COMP-03 — Competitor benchmark: Ledgerline

> SYNTHETIC FIXTURE. Assembled from public marketing pages + scripted trial walkthroughs (no credentials, no scraping). Evaluated against the same 10-task Harbor protocol as our usability sessions.

## Positioning angle: API-first invoicing with client payment links.

### Task 1 vs theme: Branch handoff drops context at the counter
A customer starts online, finishes at my window, and I know nothing. They repeat everything, slower, angrier.

Rival behavior: completes cleanly in under two minutes.

### Task 2 vs theme: Fraud alerts: ops wants more, owners feel spammed (PLANTED DIVERGENCE)
The alert never says what TO do, only what happened. Actionable would be a button, not a paragraph.

Rival behavior: completes cleanly in under two minutes.

### Task 3 vs theme: Accountant collaboration runs on screenshots
I tell every client this: give me view access and we both save three hours. Nobody does. They fear I'll see everything.

Rival behavior: passes but hides the limit until submit.

### Task 4 vs theme: Mobile deposit limits surprise growing businesses
In our old system, limits adapted quarterly. Here it's a wall I discover at speed.

Rival behavior: requires three more steps than Harbor.

### Task 5 vs theme: Invoice chasing eats the morning
The biggest invoice is never the problem. It's the twelve small ones that nobody owns.

Rival behavior: completes cleanly in under two minutes.

### Task 6 vs theme: Receipt capture dies at the point of sale
My shortcut is snapping it at the register, but half the time the photo is blurry and the app rejects it without saying why.

Rival behavior: fails the Spanish variant.

### Task 7 vs theme: Approval chains stall on one absent person
The approver sees an amount with no context. Approve what? For whom? Against which job? It's a blind signature.

Rival behavior: matches Harbor exactly — parity, not advantage.

### Task 8 vs theme: Auto-matched bank feeds: loved by owners, distrusted by accountants (PLANTED DIVERGENCE)
Train it on my corrections, visibly. If it learns my vendors, it's a colleague. If not, it's a slot machine.

Rival behavior: matches Harbor exactly — parity, not advantage.

### Task 9 vs theme: Payroll timing vs cash reality
The payroll provider debits a day early 'for processing'. That day costs me sleep and overdraft math.

Rival behavior: fails the Spanish variant.

### Task 10 vs theme: Spanish statements translate words, not meaning
The glossary contradicts the statements on three terms I counted. I stopped counting after three.

Rival behavior: fails the Spanish variant.

### Task 11 vs theme: Branch handoff drops context at the counter
Privacy theater: I can't see their online steps 'for security', but I can see their balance. Explain that logic.

Rival behavior: completes cleanly in under two minutes.

### Task 12 vs theme: Fraud alerts: ops wants more, owners feel spammed (PLANTED DIVERGENCE)
Let me snooze a merchant, not the channel. My grocery store is not a threat model.

Rival behavior: matches Harbor exactly — parity, not advantage.

## Verdict: fastest receivables flow; no payroll view; receipt capture is web-only.
